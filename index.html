FamilyWall

A private family wall calendar.

FamilyWall is a personal, non-commercial app that runs on one household's own wall display. It shows the family's Google Calendar events alongside chores, lists and meal plans that are stored only on that display.

It is not offered to the public, has no accounts or sign-ups, and has no servers of its own. It connects only to Google Calendar, using the household's own Google account.
